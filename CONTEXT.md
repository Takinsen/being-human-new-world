# Being Human New World

The **Starter Pack**: a product that gives people who have just moved into a Bangkok campus area the local know-how that maps and search engines don't carry.

## Language

**Starter Pack**:
The whole product: all the Local Know-how and Senior Stories for one Campus Area, the same for every Newcomer.
_Avoid_: Your New Home Starter Pack, guide, app (as the product name)

### People

**Newcomer** (คนมาใหม่):
Anyone who has recently moved to live in a Campus Area, regardless of where they came from. The primary persona is a first-year university student from another province, but the product never asks for or labels a Newcomer's origin.
_Avoid_: เด็กต่างจังหวัด, คนต่างจังหวัด, outsider, Target (in user-facing text)

**Senior** (รุ่นพี่):
A person who was once a Newcomer in the same Campus Area and contributes Local Know-how under their own name, face and hometown. Seniors are the only people whose hometown the product shows.
_Avoid_: Expert, contributor, admin

### Place

**Campus Area** (ย่าน):
The neighbourhood a Newcomer can reach from one university on foot or by motorcycle taxi, plus the commute routes into it. The first version covers Chulalongkorn University only (Samyan, Banthat Thong, Siam, Chula soi). It is a base, not a new home: home stays the Newcomer's hometown.
_Avoid_: Bangkok (as scope), city, New Home

**Place** (สถานที่):
A real, named spot in the Campus Area that a piece of Local Know-how can point to, so a Newcomer can find it on an ordinary map. A Place carries no know-how of its own.
_Avoid_: POI, pin, location, venue

### Content

**Local Know-how**:
Unwritten knowledge of how things work in a Campus Area and what counts as normal there (prices, procedures, etiquette, traps), as opposed to where things are. It may point to one or more Places, or none. Written either by the team (unattributed) or by a Senior (attributed).
_Avoid_: Local knowledge (too vague), location info, POI

**Senior Story**:
A Senior's short first-person account of their own first year, including the hard parts, shown so a Newcomer sees that struggling early is normal.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, review

### Categories

**Transportation** (การเดินทาง):
Know-how for moving around and into the Campus Area: modes, fares, transfers, timing.

**Everyday Food** (ของกิน):
Know-how on eating affordably in the Campus Area, including Home Taste.

**Living Alone** (อยู่คนเดียว):
Know-how for the household and health tasks a Newcomer used to have done for them: laundry, drinking water, rubbish, falling sick.
_Avoid_: Daily Life (reads as a place list)

**Study** (ที่อ่านหนังสือ):
Know-how on where a Newcomer can read or work outside class and on what terms: opening hours, whether you must order, who is let in.
_Avoid_: Library list, coworking directory

**Finding Your People** (หาเพื่อน):
Know-how on how a Newcomer finds company and support in the Campus Area: clubs and activities that welcome newcomers, and where to turn when lonely or stressed.
_Avoid_: Social, community, networking

**Home Taste** (รสชาติบ้าน):
Everyday Food know-how pointing to a Place that serves authentic food from a Thai region, vouched for by a Senior from that region. Every region is shown to every Newcomer; the Newcomer picks the region.
_Avoid_: Regional food, comfort food

**Starter Checklist**:
Local Know-how ordered by when a Newcomer needs it (first week, first month), which the Newcomer ticks off.
_Avoid_: Onboarding, tutorial
